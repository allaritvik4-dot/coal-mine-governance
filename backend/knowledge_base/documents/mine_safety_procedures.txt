COAL MINE OPERATIONAL GOVERNANCE AND FIELD SAFETY MANUAL

DOCUMENT REF: DGMS-SOP-COAL-2025/REV-03
APPLICABILITY: ALL UNDERGROUND AND OPENCAST COAL EXTRACTION DISTRICTS

================================================================================
SECTION 1: SOP-001 — PRE-SHIFT EXAMINATION AND FLAMMABLE GAS TESTING
================================================================================

1.1 OBJECTIVE & SCOPE
To establish mandatory verification protocols for underground atmospheric conditions prior to shift personnel entry, ensuring strict adherence to Coal Mines Regulations 2017 (CMR 2017) Regulations 123, 129, and 153.

1.2 RESPONSIBILITY
Certified Mining Sirdar / Overman holding valid DGMS Gas Testing Certificate.

1.3 PROTOCOL STEPS:
Step 1: Instrument Pre-Check:
- Ensure the portable multi-gas detector (DGMS approved for CH4, CO, CO2, O2, H2S) is freshly calibrated within the last 30 days. Perform bump testing using calibration gas mix before descending.
- Check battery status (minimum 80% charge required for full 8-hour shift autonomy).
- Inspect flame safety lamp (if utilized for secondary verification): check wick, gauze for tears, magnetic lock, and fuel reservoir.

Step 2: Intake Airway Verification:
- Commence inspection from the main intake splits. Measure air velocity using digital vane anemometer.
- Verify minimum airflow velocity (>= 0.5 m/s in faces, >= 1.5 m/s in main intakes).
- Record ambient Oxygen (O2 >= 19.5%) and Carbon Monoxide (CO < 5 ppm).

Step 3: Face and Roof Cavity Probing:
- Advance towards active working faces (Panel A Longwall, Bord & Pillar headings).
- Extend telescopic probe to roof level and into roof cavities where lighter methane gas accumulates.
- Check gas concentrations at 30 cm from roof line, middle height, and 30 cm from floor (for heavier gases like CO2).
- Maximum Permissible Limits:
  * Methane (CH4) < 0.5% at face before work commences.
  * If CH4 exceeds 0.75% in general body, alert overman.
  * If CH4 reaches or exceeds 1.25%: Immediately stop all machinery, isolate district electrical power at the flameproof gate-end box, prevent personnel entry, and report immediately to Mine Manager.

Step 4: Statutory Logging:
- Complete DGMS Form IV (Register of Daily Inspections) at the inbye reporting station.
- Record date, exact time, CH4 %, CO ppm, O2 %, ventilation velocity, roof condition, and signature of examining Sirdar.

SECTION 2: SOP-002 — RESIN ROOF BOLT INSTALLATION AND PULL TESTING
================================================================================

2.1 OBJECTIVE & SCOPE
To define the procedure for mechanized and manual roof bolt installation, grouting, and post-installation anchorage verification in compliance with Strata Control and Support Plans (SCSP).

2.2 MANDATORY PARAMETERS:
- High-yield deformed rebar bolts: Grade Fe-500, diameter 22 mm, nominal length 1.8 m or 2.4 m.
- Resin capsules: Polyester resin cartridges (fast-set: 15-30 seconds; slow-set: 90-120 seconds).
- Hole diameter: 28 mm to 32 mm drilled using rotary roof bolter with wet drilling or dust collector.

2.3 STEP-BY-STEP PROCEDURE:
Step 1: Sounding and Dressing:
- Prior to drilling, dress loose roof and side spalls using a telescoping steel dressing bar. Never work under unsupported ground. Always install temporary hydraulic props or friction jacks within 1.2 m of the fresh face.

Step 2: Hole Drilling:
- Align drill mast perpendicular to roof strata bedding plane (or angled at 60° over coal ribs as specified in SCSP).
- Drill hole to depth exactly equal to bolt length minus 50 mm (to accommodate bearing plate and nut).
- Flush borehole with water or air jet to remove drilling fines that impede resin bond.

Step 3: Resin Insertion and Spin:
- Insert fast-setting resin capsule first (into top anchorage horizon), followed by slow-setting capsule (lower column).
- Push bolt into hole with drill chuck adapter.
- Rotate bolt at 400-600 RPM for 15-20 seconds to thoroughly shred outer capsule skin and mix resin mastic with hardener.
- Hold bolt firmly without rotation for 30 seconds until fast resin solidifies (gel time).
- Install steel bearing plate (150 mm x 150 mm x 8 mm dished washer) and hex nut.
- Apply tightening torque of 180 to 220 N·m using calibrated pneumatic or electric torque wrench.

Step 4: Statutory Anchorage Pull-Testing:
- Frequency: At least 1 in every 10 installed bolts (10%) must be pull-tested within 24 hours of installation.
- Mount hydraulic center-hole ram and calibrated digital gauge.
- Incrementally apply tensile load up to 100 kN (10.2 tonnes).
- Pass Criteria: The bolt must sustain 80 kN tensile load with less than 6 mm total displacement, and 100 kN without anchorage rupture.
- Defective bolts failing load test must be marked with red paint and supplemented by two adjacent bolts installed within 300 mm.

SECTION 3: SOP-003 — OPENCAST BENCH DRESSING AND SLOPE STABILITY MONITORING
================================================================================

3.1 OBJECTIVE & SCOPE
Standard operating protocols for maintaining opencast bench safety, crest clearances, loose rock scaling, and continuous slope displacement detection per CMR 2017 Regulation 175.

3.2 SAFETY RULES AND CLEARANCE ZONES:
- Bench height must not exceed the maximum digging height of the excavator shovel.
- Bench width must exceed bench height and never be less than machine width plus 2.0 m.
- Safety berm height along haul edges must be >= 50% of the wheel diameter of the largest operating vehicle (e.g. 1.8 m berm for HD-785 dumper with 3.6 m tyre diameter).
- Bench face slope angle must not exceed 55 degrees unless specific geotechnical approval is granted by the DGMS.

3.3 SLOPE MONITORING AND BENCH DRESSING PROTOCOL:
Step 1: Visual and Robotic Pre-Shift Scan:
- The opencast assistant manager must visually inspect all bench crests, toes, and highwalls before shifting earth-moving equipment.
- Look specifically for: tension cracks running parallel to bench crest, bulging at the toe of the bench, perched boulders or overhangs, and water seepage along joint planes.

Step 2: Automated Geodetic Prism Monitoring:
- Ensure robotic total stations (RTS) and ground-based synthetic aperture radar (GB-SAR) are operational.
- Trigger automated prism scans every 30 minutes.
- Alert Thresholds:
  * Green (Normal): Velocity < 2 mm/day.
  * Amber (Warning): Velocity between 2 mm/day and 10 mm/day. Increase scan frequency to 10 minutes; restrict light vehicular traffic.
  * Red (Evacuation Critical): Velocity > 10 mm/day or acceleration > 1 mm/day². Immediately sound mine siren, evacuate all HEMM from bench toe and crest, barricade haul road, and notify Mine Manager.

Step 3: Scaling and Mechanical Dressing:
- Overhanging rock or perched boulders must be dressed down from above using hydraulic rock breakers or long-reach excavator booms.
- Scaling from below an overhang is strictly prohibited.
- Maintain a minimum safety buffer distance of 1.5 times the bench height from the toe while clearing fallen rocks.

SECTION 4: SOP-004 — SHOT-FIRING AND BLASTING PROTOCOLS
================================================================================

4.1 STATUTORY AUTHORIZATION
Blasting operations must be conducted solely by licensed Shot-Firers holding valid DGMS Overman's or Shot-Firer's Competency Certificates.

4.2 PROTOCOL FOR OPENCAST AND UNDERGROUND BLASTING:
Step 1: Pre-Blast Preparation and Hole Clearing:
- Check hole depths with tamping rod. Ensure holes are free of water, sludge, or blockages.
- Verify burden, spacing, and sub-grade drilling conform to approved blasting design.

Step 2: Explosive Handling and Charging:
- Only permitted explosives (DGMS approved emulsion, slurry, or ANFO) and approved delay detonators (shock tubes / non-electric detonators) may be utilized.
- Never use metal rods for tamping. Use smooth wooden or antistatic plastic tamping rods only.
- In underground coal seams, use P1 / P3 / P5 permitted explosives. Maximum charge per hole must not exceed statutory limit (1000g for solid blasting in Degree II seams).
- Fill remainder of hole with non-combustible stemming material (clay / sand mix ratio 3:1). Stemming length must be at least equal to the burden distance.

Step 3: Clearance and Danger Zone Enforcement:
- Danger Zone: Opencast blasting danger radius is 500 meters from the blasting site. Underground blasting danger zone is 150 meters along all intersecting roadways.
- Post trained guards at every access route, trail, and roadway entering the danger zone.
- Guards must carry red flags and whistles; no unauthorized person may pass under any circumstances.
- Sound siren in three distinct stages:
  * First Siren (5 minutes prior): Prolonged continuous horn — Danger Zone Clear.
  * Second Siren (1 minute prior): Intermittent horn — All personnel into shelters, prepare to fire.
  * Third Blast Signal: Shot is fired by exploder key.

Step 4: Post-Blast Inspection:
- Wait minimum 30 minutes in opencast and underground before re-entering blast area to permit toxic fumes (NOx, CO) and dust clouds to dissipate.
- Shot-firer inspects face for misfires, sockets, hanging boulders, and damaged supports.
- If misfire is detected:
  * Never pull wires or excavate misfired charge.
  * Barricade area with red warning tape.
  * Drill a relieving hole parallel to the misfired hole at a distance of not less than 300 mm.
  * Charge and fire relieving hole to detonate original charge.
- Sound continuous All-Clear siren (2 minutes duration) only after full post-blast safety verification.

SECTION 5: SOP-005 — ELECTRICAL LOCK-OUT / TAG-OUT (LOTO) AND FLP INTEGRITY
================================================================================

5.1 OBJECTIVE & MANDATE
Zero-energy state verification prior to electrical or mechanical maintenance on 3.3 kV, 550 V, and 415 V circuits, and preservation of Flameproof (FLP) enclosure ratings under CEA Mine Regulations.

5.2 ISOLATION PROCEDURE:
Step 1: Identify and Notify:
- Identify all incoming power sources. Notify the shift supervisor, electrical engineer, and working crew of pending electrical isolation.

Step 2: Switch Off and Lock:
- Open main isolator on the flameproof gate-end box or substation panel.
- Padlock the isolator handle in the open ("OFF") position using personal safety padlock. Retain personal key.
- Attach standardized Danger Tag indicating: Name of Engineer, Section, Time of Isolation, Reason for Maintenance.

Step 3: Test for Dead (Zero Energy Verification):
- Using calibrated high-voltage proximity detector or certified test probes, verify the absence of voltage on all phases.
- Discharge residual capacitive energy and apply temporary earth connections to all line terminals.

Step 4: Flameproof Enclosure Maintenance Rules:
- Before opening any FLP enclosure underground, the area must be tested for CH4 (< 0.5% required).
- FLP flange joints must be clean, mirror-smooth, free of rust, paint, or burrs.
- Check flange gap clearance using feeler gauges: maximum permissible gap is 0.4 mm for air-break apparatus and 0.5 mm for oil-break apparatus.
- Replace all high-tensile casing bolts. Ensure every bolt hole has its corresponding bolt firmly torqued. Missing or loose bolts completely invalidate flameproof certification!

SECTION 6: SOP-006 — HEAVY EARTH MOVING MACHINERY (HEMM) OPERATION & HAULAGE
================================================================================

6.1 DAILY PRE-START INSPECTION (WALK-AROUND CHECK):
Before starting dumpers (HD-785, CAT 777), excavators (EX-350, PC-1250), or dozers (D375A), the operator must complete the Pre-Start Checklist:
- Tyre condition: Check tread depth, sidewall cuts, inflation pressure (minimum 85 PSI).
- Fluid levels: Engine oil, hydraulic fluid, coolant, brake fluid.
- Safety devices check:
  * Audio Visual Alarm (AVA) operational when reverse gear engaged.
  * Horn sound pressure level >= 110 dBA.
  * Automatic Fire Detection & Suppression System (AFDSS) cylinder pressure in green zone.
  * Operator seat belt functional and worn throughout shift.
  * Retarder brake and emergency dynamic brake tested on flat surface before entering ramp.

6.2 HAUL ROAD DISCIPLINE:
- Maximum speed limits:
  * Active pit haul road: 30 km/h.
  * Loading and dumping areas: 15 km/h.
  * Sharp curves and intersection approaches: 10 km/h.
- Minimum following distance: Maintain at least 50 meters distance between dumpers in dry weather; 100 meters during rain or dusty conditions.
- Overtaking: Strictly forbidden on haul ramps, blind curves, or within 50 meters of intersections.
- Dump Area Dumping Protocol:
  * Dumpers must reverse perpendicular to dump crest.
  * Never dump without physical contact with the dump safety berm.
  * Spotter with high-visibility vest and baton must direct dumping operations during night hours.

SECTION 7: SOP-007 — WATER INRUSH DETECTION AND EMERGENCY EVACUATION
================================================================================

7.1 PREVENTIVE DRILLING:
- When working within 120 meters of waterlogged old workings or geological faults, maintain at least two advance probe holes at 15m depth, and flanking holes at 45° drilled every 5m advance.
- Signs of approaching water inrush:
  * Sweating of coal face (droplets oozing from cleavage planes).
  * Sudden drop in ambient air temperature.
  * Increase in water volume or turbidity in floor drains.
  * Hissing noise of escaping dissolved gas (H2S or CH4).
  * Rotten egg odor indicating Hydrogen Sulphide.

7.2 EMERGENCY RESPONSE TO WATER INFLOW:
- Step 1: Immediate evacuation alarm sounded via mine telephone and strobe lights.
- Step 2: All personnel immediately head towards primary escape shaft via rise workings (never evacuate down dips).
- Step 3: De-energize all non-pumping electrical feeders to prevent electrocution hazards.
- Step 4: Standby sump pumps (capacity >= 2000 GPM) automatically triggered.
- Step 5: Surface control room notified to alert DGMS regional directorate and activate mutual aid rescue brigades.

================================================================================
SECTION 8: SOP-008 — INCIDENT REPORTING AND ROOT CAUSE ANALYSIS WORKFLOW
================================================================================

8.1 MANDATORY TIMELINES UNDER MINES ACT SECTION 23:
- Fatal Accident or Serious Bodily Injury:
  * Immediate telephone/telegraphic notification within 24 hours to DGMS Director, Regional Inspector, and District Magistrate.
  * Formal Form IV-A report submitted within 72 hours.
- Dangerous Occurrence (e.g., fire, spontaneous heating, water inrush, bench failure, overwinding of cage, rope failure):
  * Notice submitted within 24 hours.

8.2 SCENE PRESERVATION & INVESTIGATION:
- The site of a fatal accident or dangerous occurrence must be sealed and preserved completely undisturbed until inspected by the DGMS Inspector.
- No machinery, debris, or supports may be moved except as strictly required for rescue or recovery of casualties.
- Root Cause Analysis (RCA) Team: Comprising Mine Manager, Safety Officer, Workmen's Inspector, and Senior Mechanical/Electrical Engineer.
- Methodology: 5-Why Analysis and Fault Tree Analysis (FTA) to establish direct causes, underlying mechanical failures, and systematic management deficiencies.
- Action Tracking: Corrective actions entered into compliance governance system with designated responsible officer and strict closure deadline.
